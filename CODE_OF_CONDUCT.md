# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in IntelliStreet to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, caste, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, patient and welcoming.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Assume good faith; focus on what is best for the project and community.
- Respect that people have different backgrounds, skills and time zones.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, derogatory comments, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Public or private harassment, trolling, or deliberate intimidation.
- Publishing others' private information (such as addresses or emails) without explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, chat) and when an individual is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the maintainers at **[manishpathania092@gmail.com]**. All reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they deem inappropriate, threatening or harmful.
